Re-layout while images load

Forty cards with images of four heights and no width/height attributes, so every card grows when its image arrives. Open DevTools, set the network to Slow 4G and reload: the list re-lays out in the next frame after an image arrives, at most once per frame, and once more after the last one (motimeline-951). ?watchImages=false turns image watching off: images then never trigger a re-layout, which is right only for lists whose images have a fixed height.
