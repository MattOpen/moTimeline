Column choice with fractional heights

Two columns whose real heights differ by 0.20 px, less than the rounding of offsetHeight. The left column ends at 201.0 px (2 × 100.5), the right one at 201.2 px, so card 4 belongs on the left. Summing rounded heights (2 × 101 = 202 against 201) picks the right column; the right float then drops into the free left column, shifted by the mirrored card margins, and leaves a gap beside it (motimeline-931).
